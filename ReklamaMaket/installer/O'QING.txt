Reklama Maket - CorelDRAW plagini
https://sunnatilla-designer.uz/

O'RNATISH
1. CorelDRAW'ni yoping.
2. install.bat ni ikki marta bosing.
   - "Windows protected your PC" chiqsa: More info -> Run anyway.
   - Ruxsat so'ralsa: Yes.
   - Yashil "O'rnatildi" yozuvi chiqsa - tayyor.
3. CorelDRAW'ni oching: Reklama -> Reklama Maket.

FAOLLASHTIRISH
4. Panelda "Kompyuter kodingiz"ni nusxalang ("Nusxa olish").
5. Kodni sunnatilla-designer.uz orqali yuboring - sizga litsenziya kaliti beriladi.
6. Kalitni panelga qo'yib, "Faollashtirish" ni bosing.
   Kalit faqat shu kompyuterda ishlaydi.

MUAMMOLAR
- Menyu ko'rinmasa: CorelDRAW'ni F8 tugmasini bosib turgan holda oching.
- O'chirish: cmd oynasida  install.bat -Uninstall
